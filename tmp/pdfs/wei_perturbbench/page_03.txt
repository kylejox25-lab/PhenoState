Fig. 1 | Workflow and datasets for benchmarking the single-cell perturbation 
effect prediction. a, We classified the single-cell perturbation effects 
prediction tasks into ‘cellular context generalization scenario’ and ‘perturbation 
generalization scenario’ . In the cellular context generalization scenario, we 
benchmarked 14 methods across 12 datasets. In the perturbation generalization 
scenario, we benchmarked 18 methods across 17 datasets. We adopted 6 metrics 
to evaluate the performance of these algorithms, and we also assessed their 
robustness, scalability and consumed computational resources. b, Detailed 
information of the 12 single-cell perturbation datasets used in cellular context 
generalization scenario. c, Detailed information of the 17 single-cell perturbation 
datasets used in perturbation generalization scenario.
